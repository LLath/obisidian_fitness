### Lower Day 1: Glute & Quad Focus

- **Barbell Hip Thrusts:** 3 sets of 8–10 reps _(heavy load, peak contraction)_

- **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets of 10–12 reps per leg _(torso leaned slightly forward to target glutes)_

- **Goblet Squats or Leg Press:** 3 sets of 8–12 reps _(feet placed higher/wider for glute bias)_

- **Seated Cable Abduction or Banded Clamshells:** 2–3 sets of 15–20 reps

Upper Day 1: Push & Pull

- **Lat Pulldowns or Pull-Ups:** 3 sets of 8–12 reps

- **Dumbbell Bench Press or Overhead Press:** 3 sets of 8–12 reps

- **Seated Cable Rows:** 3 sets of 10–15 reps

- **Dumbbell Lateral Raises:** 3 sets of 12–15 reps

Lower Day 2: Glute & Posterior Chain

- **Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs):** 3 sets of 8–10 reps _(focus on deep hip hinge and glute stretch)_

- **Barbell or Machine Glute Bridges:** 3 sets of 10–12 reps _(moderate weight, high tension)_

- **Walking Lunges or Reverse Lunges:** 3 sets of 10 reps per leg

- **Cable Kickbacks or Standing Abduction:** 3 sets of 12–15 reps per side

Upper Day 2: Push & Pull

- **Barbell or Dumbbell Rows:** 3 sets of 8–12 reps

- **Incline Dumbbell Press:** 3 sets of 8–12 reps

- **Face Pulls:** 3 sets of 15 reps

- **Biceps Curls & Triceps Extensions (Superset):** 3 sets of 10–15 reps
